Miami or Fort Lauderdale: Which Is Better for Flying to the DR?

If you live in South Florida, you have two airports for getting to the Dominican Republic: Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL). They're not the same — the airlines, the direct destinations, and especially the final price all change. Here's the real difference.

Quick Comparison

AirportDirect AirlinesApprox. Duration
MIA (Miami)American Airlines, Arajet, LATAM*2h 10min – 2h 40min
FLL (Fort Lauderdale)Spirit Airlines, American Airlines2h 15min – 2h 30min

*LATAM only covers the Punta Cana route.

American Airlines dominates Miami (MIA) — it's one of its main hubs in the southeast — with the most direct flights and destinations: Santo Domingo (SDQ), Punta Cana (PUJ), and Santiago (STI). Arajet, the Dominican low-cost carrier, also flies MIA-SDQ and MIA-PUJ, and LATAM covers MIA-PUJ. If you live north of Miami-Dade or in Broward, Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is usually closer, and there Spirit Airlines runs direct flights to Santo Domingo and Punta Cana at a low base price, plus American Airlines to Santo Domingo.

By Destination in the DR

Santo Domingo (SDQ): from Miami, American Airlines and Arajet, around 2h 25min. From Fort Lauderdale, American Airlines and Spirit Airlines, around 2h 20min.

Punta Cana (PUJ): from Miami, American Airlines, Arajet, and LATAM, around 2h 39min. From Fort Lauderdale, Spirit Airlines, around 2h 30min.

Santiago (STI): only from Miami with American Airlines, the shortest route of the group, around 2h 10min. Fort Lauderdale has no direct flight to Santiago — you'd need to connect.

When Each One Makes Sense

Miami: more frequency, more direct destinations (including Santiago), and the better option if you want flexible schedules or are traveling with large bags on a full fare. Fort Lauderdale + Spirit: the cheaper option on paper to Santo Domingo or Punta Cana, but check the extra fees carefully before deciding — the low base price doesn't always win in the end.

Tricks to Get the Best Price

Compare Miami and Fort Lauderdale in the same search for the same destination and date — sometimes a $30-40 difference doesn't justify the restrictions of a basic fare. Book on weekdays (Tuesdays or Wednesdays tend to be cheaper) and set price alerts on Google Flights or Skyscanner for your specific route.

Baggage: What Nobody Explains Clearly

Spirit is the cheapest airline on base price, but it charges extra for almost everything: carry-on, checked bag, and seat selection — the "Bare Fare" doesn't include any of that. American Airlines includes more by default on its standard fares, but its "Basic Economy" fare also restricts baggage and seating. Either way, always compare the total price with baggage included before assuming one airline is cheaper than the other.

Don't trust just the first price you see: with Spirit especially, add carry-on + checked bag + seat before comparing against American or Arajet. The real total can completely change which one is actually cheaper.

There's no "winning" airport for everyone in South Florida — it depends on where you live, which DR destination you're headed to, and whether Spirit's base price or American's flexibility works better for you. Compare both before paying, with baggage included — that's what actually saves you money.

Frequently Asked Questions About Flights from Miami to the DR

Which airport is better, Miami or Fort Lauderdale, for flying to the DR?

Miami (MIA) has more direct flights and more destinations (Santo Domingo, Punta Cana, and Santiago) because it's one of American Airlines' main hubs. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) has fewer direct routes, but Spirit Airlines usually comes out cheaper to Santo Domingo and Punta Cana.

How long is a direct flight from Miami to the Dominican Republic?

Between 2h10min and 2h40min depending on the destination: Santiago is the shortest (~2h10min), Santo Domingo is around 2h25min, and Punta Cana is about 2h39min.

Which airlines fly direct from Miami or Fort Lauderdale to the DR?

From Miami: American Airlines (Santo Domingo, Punta Cana, and Santiago), Arajet (Santo Domingo and Punta Cana), and LATAM (Punta Cana). From Fort Lauderdale: American Airlines and Spirit Airlines to Santo Domingo and Punta Cana.

Is it cheaper to fly through Fort Lauderdale with Spirit?

The base price is usually lower, but Spirit charges extra for almost everything: carry-on, checked bag, and seat selection. Always compare the total price with baggage included before assuming it's cheaper than American.

How do I get the best price on a Miami-DR flight?

Compare Miami and Fort Lauderdale in the same search, check the total price with baggage included (not just the base fare), book on weekdays, and set price alerts on Google Flights or Skyscanner for your specific route.
