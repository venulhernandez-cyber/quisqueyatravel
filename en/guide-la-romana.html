🎨 La Romana

La Romana & Casa de Campo: the Honest Guide to a Luxury Destination

If Punta Cana is the destination everyone knows, La Romana is the one almost nobody mentions until someone with real money recommends it. World-class golf, a Mediterranean village built over a river, and one of the most exclusive coastlines in the Caribbean. Here's who this destination is for, what it really costs, and whether the extra spend is worth it.

What Makes La Romana Different

La Romana isn't a cheap resort destination or a backpacker spot. It's the part of the DR chosen by serious golfers, honeymooners with a real budget, and families who want villa-level privacy instead of the crowd of a giant all-inclusive resort.

The heart of it all is Casa de Campo — 7,000 acres of resort, villas, and residential community on the southeast coast. It's not just a hotel, it's almost its own town, with a marina, tennis courts, stables, and three championship golf courses.

Worth knowing: The Teeth of the Dog course, designed by Pete Dye, is considered the best golf course in the Caribbean and one of the best in the world. If whoever's traveling with you golfs, this alone can justify the trip.

What to Do Beyond Golf

Altos de Chavón is the surprise that gets everyone — a Mediterranean-style village built in the 1980s on a cliff above the Chavón River, with stone streets, an amphitheater where artists like Frank Sinatra and Julio Iglesias have performed, and art and craft galleries. It feels more like Italy than the Dominican Republic, and that's exactly the appeal.

Isla Catalina is the classic beach escape from La Romana — white sand, coral reef close to shore, great for snorkeling. It's calmer and less crowded than Punta Cana's Isla Saona.

If you like fishing, the Casa de Campo marina runs deep-sea fishing trips — marlin, mahi-mahi, and tuna are common in these waters.

A couple staying 4 nights at Casa de Campo with one round of golf included, flights included, can expect to pay $2,500–4,500. That's more than a standard all-inclusive in Punta Cana — which is why this isn't the destination for every budget.

When to go: December through April is high season and villa prices climb considerably. If golf isn't your priority, May-June and September-October offer better rates with still-pleasant weather.

Who This Destination Is For (and Who It Isn't)

If you're after partying, nightlife, and a resort full of young people — stay in Punta Cana or Bávaro. La Romana is quiet, exclusive, and built for unplugging, playing golf, or celebrating something special like a honeymoon or anniversary.

If you're traveling with young kids, Casa de Campo's villas give you more space and privacy than a traditional all-inclusive resort, but you'll pay for it. It's worth it if the budget allows.

The Truth Nobody Tells You

La Romana isn't "cheap" by any measure, and if your idea of the DR is a beach packed with merengue and colmados on every corner, you won't find it here. This is the Dominican Republic dressed up — championship golf, art and architecture, and a level of service built for people who can pay for it.

But if you have the budget and want something different from the standard all-inclusive formula, La Romana gives you a version of the island very few tourists ever see — and it's still, at the end of the day, as Dominican as any other corner of it.

Frequently Asked Questions About La Romana

What is Casa de Campo?

Casa de Campo is a 7,000-acre resort in La Romana with villas, a marina, tennis courts, stables, and three championship golf courses, including Teeth of the Dog, considered the best golf course in the Caribbean.

What is Altos de Chavón?

It's a Mediterranean-style village built in the 1980s atop a cliff over the Chavón River, with stone streets, an amphitheater where international artists have performed, and art and craft galleries.

How much does a round of golf cost in La Romana?

A round at Teeth of the Dog costs $150-250 per person. It's one of the most prestigious golf courses in the world, designed by Pete Dye.

How much does a trip to La Romana cost?

A couple staying 4 nights at Casa de Campo with one round of golf included, flights included, can expect to pay $2,500–4,500 — more than a standard all-inclusive in Punta Cana.

Is La Romana a destination for every type of traveler?

Not exactly: it's quiet and exclusive, built for golf, honeymoons, or unplugging. If you want partying and nightlife with a young crowd, Punta Cana or Bávaro are the better fit.
